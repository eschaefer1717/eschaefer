About

I'm a first-year PhD student in computer science at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Eric Wong in the Brachio Lab. I'm interested in machine learning enhancements to surgical applications and AI safety.

I'm grateful to be supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP).

I graduated from Yale in 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in Humanities (Literature). At Yale, I served as president of my residential college, Head TA for CS50, and a recruitment coordinator and interviewer for Yale Admissions. I also make graphic design and creative work.
