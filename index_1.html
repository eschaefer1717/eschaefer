About

I'm a first-year PhD student in computer science at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Eric Wong in the Brachio Lab. I'm interested in machine learning enhancements to surgical applications and AI safety more broadly.

I'm very grateful to be supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP).

I graduated from Yale in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in Humanities (Literature Concentration). You can read my two theses here and here respectively. Beyond my academics at Yale, I served as president of my residential college, Head TA for CS50, and a recruitment coordinator and interviewer for Yale Admissions.

In my free time, I enjoy graphic design and other creative work. I'm also an avid reader and I like exploring new restaurants around the world.
